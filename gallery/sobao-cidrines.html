Sobao by Los Cidrines & La Bodeguita de Manolo

Bakery & Speakeasy

Puerto Rico · 2021–2022 · Photographer & Videographer

Sobao by Los Cidrines and La Bodeguita de Manolo share one address in San Juan. Sobao is the bakery and restaurant from Los Cidrines, an iconic Puerto Rico bakery name. The Bodeguita is the speakeasy hidden inside: a camouflaged door, moody ambiance, natural wood, exposed bulbs. I shot product photography and social video for both as one project.
